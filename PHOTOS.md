# Photos — Lemma Math Studio (Margin Notes)

One table, one light: a single daylit shoot by **Karolina Grabowska** (Kaboompics), Pexels ids
5311393–5311460 — a tutor and one student at a low wooden table in front of a grey sofa, worksheets
marked in mint and pink highlighter, orange notebooks. Every frame shows **one tutor and one student,
or the student alone**, so no photograph suggests a group class. The student is an adult.

Licence: the **Pexels License** (free to use, no attribution required; not to be sold unaltered or
used to imply endorsement). Credit is given here anyway.

These are **stock photographs, not work by the sample studio**. The site labels every frame "Sample
photo". Never describe them as Lemma's studio, its tutor, a student or a student's result. No frame
shows a grade, a score or graded work.

Processed by `concepts/tutoring-studio-margin-notes/photo-pipeline/photos.mjs` (crop + one shared
grade: the cool whites pulled a touch warmer toward the page's paper, saturation held back a little so
the highlighter greens sit beside the lime; no retouching). Only the processed AVIF/WebP crops are in
`public/photos/` — no originals.
